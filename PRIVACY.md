# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 October 2026_

MD Viewer is a Markdown reader made by Andrii Kedrov. This policy explains what the app does with your data.
Short version: **nothing leaves your computer.**

## What the app collects

Nothing. MD Viewer has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind.
We do not receive, store or sell any information about you or your documents.

## What stays on your computer

To work as a normal desktop app, MD Viewer keeps a small settings file on your own computer:

- the tabs that were open when you quit, and the list of recently opened files;
- your preferences: theme, language, page width and text size;
- on macOS, the permissions you granted to folders, so pictures and links in your documents keep working
  after a restart.

This file is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app's data or using **Clear recent files** removes it.

## Network access

The app itself does not connect to the internet. A document you open may contain pictures hosted on the web;
to show them, MD Viewer downloads them from the address written in the document, exactly like a web browser
would. Links you click open in your default browser.

## Children

The app is suitable for everyone and collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at <https://github.com/ndkedrov/md-viewer/issues>.

# Політика конфіденційності

MD Viewer нічого не збирає й нікуди не надсилає: без облікових записів, аналітики, реклами й звітів про збої.
На вашому компʼютері зберігаються лише відкриті вкладки, список нещодавніх файлів, налаштування (тема, мова,
ширина сторінки, розмір тексту) і на macOS — надані доступи до тек. Застосунок сам не виходить в інтернет;
лише картинки, на які посилається відкритий документ, завантажуються з їхніх адрес, як у браузері.
Питання — у розділі issues: <https://github.com/ndkedrov/md-viewer/issues>.
